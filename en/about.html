About

Background

For six years at Clermont Foot, I was a member of the first-team staff, where I founded and led the video and data analysis unit. I learned there to put data at the service of a coaching staff, on the pitch and at the pace of a season.

I am self-taught in data analysis and software development, through personal and professional experience. I have since started a recognition-of-prior-learning process (French VAE) towards the engineering degree in mathematics and artificial intelligence at Polytech Clermont: the experience portfolio is being written, and the defence will follow. DFCO Analytics, in production at Dijon Football Côte d'Or, and the UNECATEF Data Masterclass are the direct continuation of that work.

Between two football projects, I turned the spreadsheets that had tracked my budget for fifteen years into a real application, then realised it could serve other families. The sole proprietorship brings these three activities together: training, data consulting and analysis, custom software.

How I work

  • Frequent releases. Changes reach production in small, visible, reversible steps.
  • Automated tests. Every change runs through a test suite before deployment.
  • Monthly security audits. Dependencies, configuration and access rights are reviewed every month.
  • AWS hosting. Infrastructure in the Paris region, daily backups, encryption of sensitive data.
  • GDPR. Record of processing activities, defined retention periods, documentation handed over to the client.
