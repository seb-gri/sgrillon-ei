Professional training in data and AI for sport

Giving coaches and technical staff the foundations to understand what data and artificial intelligence can do today, what they cannot, and how to work with data experts.

Current engagements

Since the 2025-26 season

Data Masterclass, UNECATEF

A course co-created for UNECATEF, the French national union of football coaches and technical directors, delivered over three half-days. It gives coaches the fundamentals to understand the possibilities and limits of data and AI, and to become active participants by learning how to work with data experts.

Role: co-designer and session facilitator, still delivering sessions on an ad hoc basis.

From the 2026-27 season

BEPF / UEFA Pro, French Football Federation

A four-hour module within the BEPF, the French Football Federation's professional coaching diploma (UEFA Pro licence). Same objective as the Data Masterclass, in a shorter format.

Role: designer and facilitator.

What participants learn

  • What data and AI can do in football today, and what they cannot do yet.
  • The main families of data (events, tracking, physical data) and their practical uses for a coaching staff.
  • How to read an analysis report, question it and spot its limits.
  • How to brief an analyst or a data provider clearly.
  • How to bring data into match preparation and review without losing on-pitch expertise.

Audit and tailored training

For clubs and organisations, I also step in before the training itself: an audit of how teams work identifies what can be improved, with or without artificial intelligence. A tailored course is then built from those findings. For analyst profiles, the programme can go as far as hands-on work: Python, applied statistics, dashboards.

Who it is for

  • Coaches and technical staff of clubs and national teams.
  • Performance analysts and video analysts.
  • Sports science students and coaching diploma candidates.
  • Training providers wishing to add a data and AI module.

Formats

On-site

At your premises, for a group from your organisation, using your data and your tools.

Remote

Live online sessions in half-day blocks.

Tailored

A programme built with you, from an audit or a specific need.
