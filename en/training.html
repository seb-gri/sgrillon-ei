Professional training in sports data analytics

Learn to collect, process and present performance and match data, with tools you keep after the course.

Curriculum

  • Python for data analysis: getting started, pandas and matplotlib, cleaning and joining datasets.
  • Applied statistics for performance: descriptive indicators, variability, trends, critical reading of a result.
  • Dashboards: choosing indicators, designing reports a coaching staff can read, automating updates.
  • Performance data: training load, GPS, physical testing, individual and team monitoring.
  • Match data: events, provider statistics, comparisons across teams and matchdays.
  • Hands-on work on your own data, with its confidentiality preserved.

Who it is for

  • Coaching staffs of clubs and national teams.
  • Performance analysts and video analysts.
  • Sports science students.
  • Training providers wishing to add a data analytics module.

Formats

On-site

At your premises, for a group from your organisation, using your data and your tools.

Remote

Live online sessions in half-day blocks, with exercises between sessions.

Tailored

A programme built with you around a specific need: a tool, a type of data, an internal project.
