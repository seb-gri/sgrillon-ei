Projects

Four projects I design, build and operate. One is in production in professional football; the others are in development or in preparation.

In production since early 2026

Training Analytics

Web platform for managing and analysing training sessions, used daily by a professional coach and their staff. The coach is the customer; I am the software vendor.

Highlights

  • Live mode for pitch-side data entry, working offline and syncing once the network is back.
  • Automated statistical reports after every session.
  • League tracking based on Genius Sports data.
  • Encryption of sensitive data, GDPR compliance.
Stack
Django and Python, PostgreSQL, Docker
Hosting
AWS: EC2 (Paris region), S3

In development

Football Tag

Online football match video sequencing and analysis tool, hosted on AWS.

Features

  • Upload a match video.
  • Set up a custom sequencing grid.
  • Sequence the match and organise the debrief.
  • Individual and team statistics across the sequencing of several matches.

Initially, Football Tag is used to build the training set for the deep learning match sequencing project described below. A version for the general public will follow.

Hosting
AWS
Status
In development, internal use

Coming soon

Family wealth tracker

Web application for tracking a family's finances, hosted on AWS, launching in November 2026.

Features

  • Account and budget forecast tracking.
  • Wealth tracking: real estate, private equity, cryptocurrencies and other assets.
  • One user account per family member.
  • Automatic alerts.

Over time, an AI assistant could support children's financial education and suggest improvements.

Background

I have tracked my own budget for almost fifteen years, first in a spreadsheet extended every year with more advanced features. The web application builds on that logic, to share it with family and friends first, then offer it at a larger scale.

Hosting
AWS
Status
Launch planned for November 2026

In preparation

Match sequencing with deep learning

Research and development project: predicting the sequencing of a football match from event and tracking data with a deep learning model. This sequencing opens an angle of match and team analysis that does not exist today.

The model is trained on matches sequenced manually with Football Tag. An analysis platform will then make the results available to clubs on a subscription basis.

Stack
Sequence deep learning, Python, Amazon SageMaker for training, batch inference on AWS
Timeline
Data preparation in early 2027, experiments from March 2027, first version of the platform targeted for late 2027
